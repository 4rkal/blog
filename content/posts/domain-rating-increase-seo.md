Recently I was interested in improving the search engine ranking of my crypto exchange aggregator [cyphergoat.com](https://cyphergoat.com).

My strategy was simple. Acquire as many backlinks as I can. My strategy proved to be much more successfull than I expected. Ahrefs reports a 27 DR with only ~80 referring domains, which is interesting to say the least. 

The way that I was able to get backlinks from high authority websites was simple. As a crypto exchange aggregator we support ~60 coins and ~15 exchanges, so getting listed on those websites where the best way to increase my domain rating.

Most cryptocurrencies want to show where you can buy their cryptocurrency and give you a backlink. 

### Specifics
The backlink with the highest domain rating are three no follow backlinks from our github  repos and profile [github.com/cyphergoat](https://github.com/cyphergoat). 

After that we have 13 no follow backlinks from [hackernoon.com](https://hackernoon.com) (88 DR) that come from a couple of articles I published that mentioned cyphergoat. When an article is translated to multiple languages more backlinks are "created".

We also managed to get 42 Do-follow backlinks from [ethereumclassic.org](https:/ethereumclassic.org) (72 DR), since we were listed under the exchange section and it is translated to loads of languages. 

From our supported coins and exchanges we got:
1 Do follow backlink from [piratechain.com](https://piratechain.com) (48 DR)
3 Do follow backlinks from [bitcoinvn.io](https://bitcoinvn.io) (52 DR)
1 Do follow backlink from [zano.org](https://zano.org) (51 DR)
1 Backlink from [firo.org](https://firo.org) (not yet found at ahrefs)

Some directories where I got backlinks from where:
15 Do follow backlinks from [promoteproject.org](https://promoteproject.org) (43 DR)
2 Do follow backlinks from [kycnot.me](https://kycnot.me) (50 DR)
8 Do follow backlinks from [monerica.com](https://monerica.com) (39 DR)

My website got mentioned in a couple of niche "news" websites:
1 Dofollow backlink from [monero.observer](https://monero.observer) (34 DR)
1 Dofollow backlink from [revuo-xmr.com](https://revuo-xmr.com) (32 DR)

Obviously most of these backlinks are kind of niche but the idea is to submit to relevant websites in your startups niche. 

In order to get listed on most of these websites I had to manually contact them and to some I even created a PR on their website repos. 

Obviously with 60 coins and 15 exchanges I could get many more backlinks and I still have a lot of work to do.
